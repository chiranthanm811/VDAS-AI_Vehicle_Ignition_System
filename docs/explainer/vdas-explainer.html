How does the AI-based license-aware vehicle ignition system work?

Long image

A

Answer

The system protects vehicle ignition through a five-stage biometric and sensory verification pipeline. The engine relay remains locked until hardware presence, stereo 3D liveness, blink dynamics, and license validity succeed.

  1. Seat presence sensing ensures an occupant is physically seated before computer vision pipelines activate.
  2. Stereo depth disparity and a four-state blink detector reject 2D photos, digital screens, and static masks.
  3. Real-time facial recognition matches the driver against cached credentials and the official RTO database.
  4. An ESP32 microcontroller enforces fail-safe relay actuation and reports serial telemetry to the web dashboard.

What sequence authorizes the vehicle ignition?

  • driver → esp32 · Sits in driver seat (FSR)
  • esp32 → laptop · SEAT:1 serial event
  • driver → cams · Presents face to dual cameras
  • cams → laptop · Left and right video frames
  • laptop → esp32 · CMD:UNLOCK authenticated
  • esp32 → relay · GPIO25 LOW activates relay
  • esp32 → driver · LCD confirms access granted
  1. Seat Presence

    FSR sensor on GPIO34 detects driver weight.

  2. Stereo Disparity

    Dual cameras measure nose-tip disparity above 3.2 percent.

  3. Blink Dynamics

    Four-state machine verifies eye blinks via EAR.

  4. Identity Match

    Face recognition matches local cache and RTO database.

  5. Relay Actuation

    ESP32 energizes relay on GPIO25 and confirms unlock.
